# SFC campus concept art

- Tool: built-in image_gen
- Reference: `sfc-map.png`
- Output: `sfc-campus-concept.png`

## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: campus illustration for the right-hand image in the Access section of a university research lab website.
Input image: the provided sfc-map.png is the source campus aerial photograph and spatial reference.
Primary request: Transform this aerial photograph of Keio University Shonan Fujisawa Campus into serene architectural concept art.
Preserve the recognizable oblique aerial viewpoint and the actual arrangement and proportions of the campus: central white buildings and steps, pond to the left, rows of smaller white academic buildings behind, the tree-lined approach extending into the foreground, wooded surroundings and the curved-roof hall to the right.
Style/medium: refined architectural competition illustration, precise understated building edges, matte ivory architectural models integrated with softly painted natural foliage, subtle watercolor and fine paper texture, beautifully restrained detail.
Lighting/mood: quiet early morning, soft diffuse daylight, delicate atmospheric haze, gentle shadows, contemplative stillness.
Color palette: warm whites, pale sage, muted forest greens, soft gray-green water, harmonizing with a white and dark-green website.
Composition: landscape 3:2 framing, the central campus remains legible at small web display sizes, generous natural wooded margins, no border.
Constraints: retain site identity and building layout from the reference; do not invent landmark structures. No text, labels, map pins, logos, people, dramatic effects, or watermarks. Produce only the finished artwork.
